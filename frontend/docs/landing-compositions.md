# Landing composition direction

Use Gradient Waves only. Keep the original headline, supporting sentence, and two actions, left aligned. Do not add preview cards, diagrams, labels, or other content to the hero.

The website uses Midnight Blue everywhere, including navigation, bento cards, documentation, sandbox, and hosted checkout. Only the Gradient Waves artwork uses Ice. Its colors are explicitly scoped to the renderer and its static fallback; they never change the global palette.

Keep the stronger wave crests and fine-grain texture. Fade the artwork naturally into the Midnight background and keep the text readable. Respect reduced motion and stop rendering offscreen or in a hidden tab.

There are no palette or background switches and no saved appearance preferences. On phones, keep the text and actions on the left with visible waves beneath them.
