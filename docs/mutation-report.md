> directory and overwrite each other's `src/`. Halmos could also silently skip its proofs on stale build artifacts.

>   warps and rolls to symbolic values, and both are killed.

> Re-run `python3 sdk/contracts/test/mutation/run.py` with the fixed runner for clean numbers.
